5. Hazardous and special-disposal items

Chemicals, solvents, paint, oil, fuel, propane, batteries, pesticides, anything with a hazard label. These don't go in the contractor bags or to the curb.

Let her know they can't go out with general trash, and recommend we take them to the dump. We'll send her the receipt for the disposal fee.

"These can't go out with the regular trash — the county can fine for that. Our recommendation is we take them to the dump and dispose of them properly, and we'll send you the receipt for the fee."

If she agrees, load it. If she'd rather keep it on site, group and label it in one spot and let Jhen know. Send the dump receipt to Jhen same day.
